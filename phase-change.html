Key Concepts & Physics Notes:

Sloped Lines (Q = mcΔT)
  • Specific Heat Capacity (c).
  • Temperature changes steadily.
  • Heat is absorbed or released.
  • Single phase only (Solid / Liq / Gas).
Melting / Freezing (Q = mL_f)
  • Specific Latent Heat of Fusion (L_f).
  • Temperature remains constant.
  • Overcoming or forming lattice bonds.
  • Two phases coexist: Solid + Liquid.
Boiling / Condensing (Q = mL_v)
  • Specific Latent Heat of Vaporization (L_v).
  • Temperature remains constant.
  • Overcoming intermolecular bonds completely.
  • Two phases coexist: Liquid + Gas.
Transitions & Processes
  • Heating Curve: Solid → Liquid → Gas.
  • Cooling Curve: Gas → Liquid → Solid.
  • X-axis displays accumulated Heat Energy (Q).
