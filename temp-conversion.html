Live Conversion Steps for 0.00 °C

Reference Axis: TC
1. Kelvin Conversion K
Formula: K = °C + 273.15
K = (0.00) + 273.15
= 273.15 K

The Kelvin scale is an absolute thermodynamic temperature scale and does not use the degree symbol (°).

2. Fahrenheit Conversion °F
Formula: °F = (1.8 × °C) + 32
°F = ( 1.8 × 0.00 ) + 32
°F = 0.00 + 32
= 32.00 °F

The factor 9/5 (or 1.8) accounts for the 180° interval between freezing and boiling on Fahrenheit versus 100° on Celsius.

Celsius ⇄ Kelvin:

• K = °C + 273.15

• °C = K - 273.15

Celsius ⇄ Fahrenheit:

• °F = (9/5 × °C) + 32

• °C = 5/9 × (°F - 32)

Kelvin ⇄ Fahrenheit:

• K = [5/9 × (°F - 32)] + 273.15

• °F = [9/5 × (K - 273.15)] + 32
